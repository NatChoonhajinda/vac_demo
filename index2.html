SCORAD

SCORing Atopic Dermatitis

A clinical tool for assessing the severity (extent, intensity) of atopic dermatitis as objectively as possible. Total score ranges from 0 to 103.

A — Area (20% of the score)

For each body region, select how much of that region is affected by eczema. Each region is weighted by its share of body surface area (BSA).

A (extent)—/ 100

B — Intensity (60% of the score)

A representative area of eczema is selected. In this area, the intensity of each of the following signs is assessed as none (0), mild (1), moderate (2) or severe (3).

B (intensity)—/ 18

C — Subjective symptoms (20% of the score)

Using a visual analogue scale where 0 is no itch (or no sleeplessness) and 10 is the worst imaginable itch (or sleeplessness).

C (subjective)—/ 20
